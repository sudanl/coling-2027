## Venue

### Galaxy International Convention Center

Address: Galaxy Macau, Cotai, Macau  
Website: [galaxymacau.com/mice/gicc](https://www.galaxymacau.com/mice/gicc/meetings-conferences/)

Galaxy International Convention Center (GICC) is the latest addition to Galaxy Entertainment Group's ever-expanding integrated resort precinct and will usher in a new era for the MICE industry in Macau.

GICC — Asia's most iconic and advanced MICE destination — is a world-class event venue featuring 40,000 sqm of total flexible MICE space that can cater for up to 8,500 banquet guests, or cocktail parties for as many as 16,000 guests. The 10,000-sqm pillar-less exhibition hall located on the ground floor can accommodate up to 7,000 delegates.

Conveniently located within GICC, the 16,000-seat Galaxy Arena is the largest indoor arena in Macau and where spectacular events come to life — from world concert tours to thrilling fight nights, every guest at Galaxy Arena will enjoy 360° unobstructed views and high quality surround sound.

## What to do in Macau

View the Vibrant Macao at [macaotourism.gov.mo](https://www.macaotourism.gov.mo/en/).

Located on the western bank of the Pearl River Delta in southern Guangdong Province, People's Republic of China, Macao adjoins the Mainland city of Zhuhai and lies some 60 kilometres to the west of Hong Kong. Macao comprises Macao Peninsula, Taipa and Coloane. Macao Peninsula is the hub of the territory and is connected to Taipa by three bridges.

The Portuguese arrived and settled in Macao in the mid 16th Century. Thus, the city's architecture, art, religion, traditions, food and community reflect the integration of Chinese, Western and Portuguese cultures. Macao became a Special Administrative Region (SAR) of the People's Republic of China on 20th December 1999. In 2005, the Historic Centre of Macao was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List.

Macao is eight hours ahead of Greenwich Mean Time (GMT+8). Chinese and Portuguese are the official languages while Cantonese is most widely spoken. English is generally used in trade, tourism and commerce.

### Currency

- Pataca (MOP) is the official currency, linked to HKD. MOP 8.00 ≈ USD 1.00.
- Exchange available at airports, piers, hotels, and banks.
- UnionPay, Visa, Mastercard, AmEx and JCB are widely accepted. Alipay is becoming common.

### Water & Electricity

- Purified water is supplied from Mainland China. Distilled drinking water is provided in all hotels and restaurants.
- Electricity: 230V, 50Hz. Power plugs are three-pin (square-shaped).

### Healthcare

- For medical treatment or consultation, visit [Macao Tourism Healthcare Info](https://www.macaotourism.gov.mo/en/travelessential/before-you-travel/practical-info?tab=healthcare-and-hospitals).

### Climate & Clothing

- Temperate year-round. Average temperature ~23°C (73°F), ranging from 20°C to 26°C.
- Average relative humidity: 79%.
- Winter (December - March) can be cool — bring a jacket or overcoat.

### Telephone Service

- Country code: 853. Outgoing international code: 00.
